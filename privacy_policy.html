Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026 | Last Updated: August 28, 2026

Welcome to PlayVitals (the "Application"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

The Application is developed as an offline-first, ads-free gaming companion utility. Our primary focus is hardware telemetry tracking and gaming session optimization.

1. Information We Collect & How We Use It

A. Device Performance Telemetry (Local Only)

To provide real-time floating overlay status (HUD) and session reports, the Application reads system telemetry including:

  • RAM Status: Used to display free memory and execute the optional 1-tap memory boost utility.
  • Network Latency (Ping): Measured locally to help you understand your connection quality during gameplay.
  • Battery Temperature & Status: Monitored to warn you of overheating and protect your hardware health.
  • Screen Refresh Rate: Displayed to verify active display refresh capabilities.

This data is processed locally on your device in real-time and is never shared, sold, or transmitted to external servers without your explicit action.

B. Installed Gaming Application List

The Application utilizes package query parameters to let you organize and launch games. We scan installed applications solely to build your gaming library page and launch them inside our Pro Booster environment. No list of your installed apps is collected or shared externally.

C. Cloud Sync (Optional Firebase Integration)

If you explicitly choose to sync your gaming insights and settings to the cloud, the application leverages Firebase Firestore and Google Sign-In via Jetpack Credential Manager. This data is associated only with your authenticated account and is stored securely in Firebase cloud infrastructure to allow multi-device restore.

2. Android System Permissions

To deliver its core features, the Application requires the following standard Android permissions:

  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Draw over other apps): Required to show the floating telemetry HUD overlay on top of active gameplay.
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Required to scan installed apps so you can add games to your launcher library.
  • INTERNET: Required to query network ping for latency measurements, use Gemini AI recommendations, and connect to secure Firebase cloud sync.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to display the background overlay service control notification and critical temperature alerts.

3. Gemini AI Analysis Recommendations

Our hardware tuning recommendations are generated locally or securely processed via the official Google Gemini SDK. No personal information is submitted to Gemini models. We only analyze raw hardware specifications (GPU and CPU architecture names) to suggest optimal graphics guides.

4. Data Retention and Safety

All local gaming history (reflex scores, session logs) is stored in a private SQLite database via Room. You can erase this data at any time by clearing the Application's cache or storage in your system settings. Cloud synced data can be completely managed/removed upon request.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.
